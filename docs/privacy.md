# Updraft privacy policy

Updraft does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal data. It has no ads, analytics,
accounts or internet access, and asks for no permissions.

It keeps your best score and settings in two small files on your phone only; uninstalling the
app deletes them.

The **Send feedback** button (Settings > About) opens your own email app with a new message to
bartbasi@gmail.com. The draft names the game's version, your phone's model and its Android
version so that problems can be looked into; you can change or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
The **Source code** button opens the project's page in your browser.

Contact: bartbasi@gmail.com
