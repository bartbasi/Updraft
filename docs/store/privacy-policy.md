# Updraft privacy policy

Updraft is a game made by Play, Inc. This policy says what Updraft does with information about
you. The short version: it collects nothing.

Effective 17 October 2026.

## What Updraft collects

Nothing. Updraft has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect,
send or share any information about you or your phone, with us or with anyone else.

## What Updraft saves on your phone

To work as a game, Updraft keeps a few small files in its own private storage on your phone:

- your settings (balloon or paper plane, tilt sensitivity, sound on or off, climb and dive
  swapped or not)
- your best score on the Course
- your Endless progress: whether Endless is open, your best distance, your stars, the looks you
  have bought with stars and are wearing, and your daily flight

These files never leave your phone. Other apps can't read them. They are deleted when you
uninstall Updraft, and you can clear them at any time under Settings > Apps > Updraft > Storage
> Clear data (the exact names vary a little between phones).

## Internet and permissions

Updraft doesn't use the internet and asks for no permissions.

Three buttons hand something to another app on your phone, and the game itself sends nothing:

- **Send feedback** (in About) opens your own email app with a new message to us. The draft
  names the game's version, your phone's model and its Android version, so that problems can be
  looked into. You can change or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
- **Source code** (in About) opens the game's source code page on GitHub in your browser.
- **Buy me a coffee** (on the title screen and in About) opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser.
  Anything you do there is between you and Buy Me a Coffee, and their privacy policy applies.

## Google Play

If you got Updraft from Google Play, Google handles the download and any information it collects
about it under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children

Updraft collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the
change will be noted in the game's release notes on Google Play.
